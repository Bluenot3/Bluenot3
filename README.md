## About

I build agentic AI systems, multimodal interfaces, and automation infrastructure designed for real-world deployment.

I am the founder of **ZEN AI Co.**, where I develop AI products and learning systems at the intersection of education, enterprise operations, and emerging digital infrastructure. My work focuses on reducing the distance between idea and execution through deployable tools, scalable workflows, and high-leverage AI experiences.

I also designed and led what I position as the **first successful youth AI literacy program in United States history**, enabling students ages 11–18 to build and deploy real, cloud-hosted AI agents through hands-on, deployment-first learning.
