## ⟁ About

I build agentic AI systems, multimodal interfaces, and automation infrastructure designed for real-world deployment.

I am the founder of **ZEN AI Co.**, where I develop AI products, learning systems, and operational tools at the intersection of education, enterprise automation, AI infrastructure, and emerging digital credentials. My work focuses on compressing the distance between idea and execution through deployable agents, scalable workflows, and practical AI experiences people can actually use.

I also designed and led what I position as the **first successful youth AI literacy program in United States history**, enabling students ages 11–18 to build and deploy real, cloud-hosted AI agents through hands-on, deployment-first learning.
