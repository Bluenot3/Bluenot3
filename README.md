## ⟡ SYSTEM PROFILE

Alex Leschik is the founder of ZEN AI Co. and an architect of large-scale AI systems driving real-world adoption across education, enterprise operations, and emerging digital infrastructure. He designed and led the first successful youth AI literacy program in United States history, enabling students ages 11–18 to build and deploy real, cloud-hosted AI agents in partnership with the largest youth nonprofit network globally.

His work extends into early-stage on-chain AI system design, contributing to token-gated community model architectures alongside leading AI blockchain infrastructure, positioning ZEN at the convergence of AI, distributed systems, and programmable ownership.

Across enterprise environments and the ZEN ecosystem, he develops agentic systems, multimodal intelligence platforms, and operational AI frameworks engineered to compress execution, scale capability, and expand access to high-leverage technologies.
